# INTERNATIONAL GRANT AGREEMENT

**Version {{version_label}}**

---

This Grant Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**{{grantor_legal_name}}**, a California nonprofit corporation recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, with offices at {{grantor_address}} (the "**Grantor**"); and

**{{grantee_legal_name}}**, a charitable organization organized under the laws of {{grantee_country}}, with offices at {{grantee_address}} (the "**Grantee**").

## Recitals

A. The Grantor is organized and operated exclusively for charitable purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3), including {{grantor_exempt_purpose}}.

B. The Grantor has determined, in the exercise of its own discretion, that providing funds to the Grantee for the Charitable Purpose described below furthers the Grantor's own exempt purposes.

C. The Grantor retains full discretion and control over the use of the grant funds and is not acting as an agent, conduit, or intermediary for any donor or third party.

## 1. Grant Funds and Disbursement

1.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Grantor may, from time to time and in its sole discretion, provide the Grantee with grant funds (the "**Grant**") in such amounts and on such schedule as the Grantor determines. The amount of the Grant is **not fixed** and may vary over time — for example, as donations designated in support of the Grantee's work are received. The Grantor is under **no obligation** to provide any minimum or particular amount of funding, and this Agreement does not entitle the Grantee to any specific sum.

1.2 Each disbursement is conditioned on the Grantee's continued compliance with this Agreement, including its reporting obligations. The Grantor may **withhold, reduce, suspend, or discontinue** any funding at any time in its sole discretion.

## 2. Charitable Purpose and Restriction on Use

2.1 The Grantee shall use the Grant **solely** for charitable purposes that further the Grantor's own exempt purposes — namely, supporting the charitable, religious, and humanitarian work carried out by or through the Grantee and its personnel (the "**Charitable Purpose**").

2.2 The Grant may **not** be used for: any non-charitable purpose; any activity that would constitute lobbying or intervention in a political campaign under U.S. law; any private benefit to individuals except as reasonable compensation for services rendered to the Charitable Purpose; or any purpose prohibited under Section 4 below.

2.3 The Grant is **not** general, unrestricted operating support. Any proposed change to the Charitable Purpose requires the Grantor's **prior written approval**.

## 3. Grantee Representations and Covenants

The Grantee represents, warrants, and covenants that:

3.1 It is organized and operated exclusively for charitable purposes and would qualify as a charitable organization described in Section 501(c)(3) if it were a U.S. entity (it is not a private foundation or its equivalent), or it will otherwise use the Grant only for charitable purposes described in Section 170(c)(2)(B).

3.2 It will use the Grant only for the Charitable Purpose and in accordance with this Agreement.

3.3 It will keep complete and accurate books and records relating to the Grant and will maintain the Grant funds in a manner that allows them to be **traced and accounted for** separately from other funds.

## 4. Legal Compliance (Anti-Terrorism, Sanctions, Anti-Bribery, AML)

4.1 The Grantee will comply with all applicable U.S. and local laws, including the U.S. Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions programs, the USA PATRIOT Act, the Bank Secrecy Act and anti-money-laundering laws, and the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and applicable anti-bribery laws.

4.2 The Grantee represents that neither it, nor (to its knowledge) its directors, officers, or key employees, is identified on any U.S. government list of sanctioned, blocked, or prohibited parties, and it will not transfer any Grant funds to any such party.

4.3 No portion of the Grant will be used to support, directly or indirectly, terrorism, violence, money laundering, bribery, or any unlawful activity.

## 5. Reporting

5.1 The Grantee will provide the Grantor with written reports on the use of the Grant **{{reporting_schedule}}**, including a **financial accounting** of how funds were expended, using the Grantor's online salary-use report form.

5.2 The Grantee will provide a **final report** within **90 days** after the Grant funds are fully expended or the Term ends, whichever is earlier.

5.3 The Grantee will provide such additional information and supporting documentation as the Grantor may reasonably request.

## 6. Records, Inspection, and Audit

6.1 The Grantee will retain all records relating to the Grant for at least **7 years** after the end of the Term.

6.2 The Grantor and its authorized representatives may **inspect and audit** the Grantee's records relating to the Grant and may conduct **site visits**, on reasonable notice.

## 7. Discretion, Control, and Return of Funds

7.1 The Grantor retains full discretion and control over the Grant. Grant funds remain dedicated to the Charitable Purpose until expended in accordance with this Agreement.

7.2 The Grantee will **return to the Grantor**, on demand: (a) any Grant funds not used for the Charitable Purpose; (b) any funds used in breach of this Agreement; and (c) any unexpended funds remaining at the end of the Term or upon termination.

## 8. Term, Suspension, and Termination

8.1 This Agreement begins on the date of the Grantee's electronic acceptance (the "**Effective Date**") and continues in effect until terminated by either party as provided below (the "**Term**"), unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

8.2 The Grantor may **suspend or terminate** this Agreement and any further disbursements immediately if the Grantee breaches this Agreement, if the Grantor determines the funds are not being used for the Charitable Purpose, or if continued funding would be unlawful or inconsistent with the Grantor's exempt status. Sections 6, 7, and 9 survive termination.

## 9. Relationship of the Parties

The Grantee is an independent organization. Nothing in this Agreement creates an agency, partnership, employment, joint venture, or conduit relationship between the parties. The Grantee may not represent that it acts on behalf of the Grantor.

## 10. General Provisions

10.1 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing agreed to by both parties.

10.2 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Grant and supersedes any prior understandings.

10.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

10.4 **Notices.** Notices will be sent to the addresses above (or to **{{signer_email}}**) and are effective when received.

10.5 **Governing Law and Dispute Resolution.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **California, USA**, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement that the parties cannot resolve informally will be settled by **binding arbitration in California** under the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association, and judgment on the arbitration award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction.

10.6 **Electronic Acceptance.** The parties agree this Agreement may be executed and accepted **electronically**, including by the Grantee's affirmative online acceptance, and that such electronic acceptance is valid, binding, and has the same effect as a handwritten signature. The Grantee consents to transact electronically and confirms it can access and retain a copy of this Agreement.
