Foreign Grant Compliance

Tools for disbursing funds to foreign charities while preserving discretion and control, avoiding conduit treatment, and meeting sanctions and board-governance obligations. Each step below produces an immutable, hash-stamped record that links to the prior step, forming a traceable evidence chain per grantee.

This system documents a compliance process and produces evidence; it does not provide legal or tax advice. Attestation wording, voting rules, governing law and sanctioned-jurisdiction questions are configured with your counsel.
