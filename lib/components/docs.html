Documentation

Welcome to the Ergatas documentation. Pick a topic from the menu on the left, or start with one of the guides below.

Everyday tools

Available to everyone on their Dashboard.

  • Using Your Dashboard — a tour of the dashboard menu and home summary.
  • Saved Searches — save, re-run, share, and delete searches.
  • Favorites — collect and share profiles you care about.
  • Prayers and Updates — post updates and prayer requests.
  • Donations — read, filter, and export donations.

For organization administrators

Extra tools that appear for organization administrators.

  • Managing Your Organization — organization details, sharing link, and donation form.
  • Managing Worker Profiles — add, edit, invite, and remove worker profiles.
  • Ministry Review — request and submit periodic budgets and reports.

For grantees

Guides for foreign charities receiving a grant.

  • Accepting Your Grant Agreement — review, sign, and accept your grant agreement online.
  • Salary-Use Reports — certify how grant funds were applied to salaries.

Grant compliance

Tools for grant staff and board members running the foreign-grant lifecycle.

  • Grant Compliance Overview — the lifecycle and how the records link together.
  • OFAC & Sanctions Screening — screen grantees and record due diligence.
  • Board Review & Approval — record the board's decision, finding, and votes.
  • Managing Grant Agreements — publish and version the agreement grantees accept.
  • Disbursements — track releases against the delegated scope.
  • Salary-Use Reports — review the reports grantees file.
