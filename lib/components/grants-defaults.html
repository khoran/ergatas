Annual salary reports

Grantees report once a year on the calendar year just ended. The report for each year is due on this date of the following year. Reminder emails go to the organization's managers and the person who accepted the agreement, starting 30 days before the due date and every 14 days after that, up to 4 times, until the report is filed.
