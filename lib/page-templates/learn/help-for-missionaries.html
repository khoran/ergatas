Raising Support? You Don't Have to Do It Alone.

Ergatas connects missionaries with new ministry partners — completely free.

Our passion is helping missionaries reach full funding and stay there. Whether you're just getting started or you need a boost, we want to help.

How do we help? We work hard to connect you with people who are already looking for a ministry like yours — by country, people group, sending organization, or even job skill.

What does it cost? Nothing. Creating a profile is free, and we never take a cut of your donations.
