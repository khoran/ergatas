Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ergatas really free?

Yes. Creating a profile is free, and we take no platform fee and no cut of your donations. Gifts are collected and passed to your sending organization designated for your support; only bank fees are deducted, and donors can choose to cover those so 100% reaches your ministry.

How is this different from GoFundMe?

Ergatas is built for missionary support raising rather than one-off campaigns. There are no platform fees, donations flow through your sending agency so they stay tax-deductible and accountable, and supporters actively search Ergatas to find a missionary to partner with — so you get found instead of having to drive all your own traffic.
