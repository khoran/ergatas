Ergatas gives churches and mission agencies a platform to showcase their workers. Each worker gets a detailed profile where people can really get to know their ministry — and every profile links to your organization's own donation page, so support flows exactly where it should. Collect all those profiles onto one landing page you can share anywhere, or embed directly in your own website.

Completely free. We don't charge anything for profiles or display pages, and we don't take any part of donations.

  • Stripe Connect — receive on-site donations directly into your own Stripe account, with no second transfer fee. Learn more.
