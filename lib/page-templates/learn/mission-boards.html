Ergatas for Missions Committees

Find Kingdom workers who fit your church's passions and missions strategy.

Your church has a missions strategy. Ergatas helps you find the workers who fit it.

Most churches only hear about the missionaries who happen to contact them. Ergatas lets your missions committee search proactively — across many sending organizations at once — for workers serving the countries, people groups, and ministries your church is already committed to.

Our service is free for your committee and free for the workers. We don't take any part of donations made.

How does it work?

1

Workers create profiles
Missionaries from many different organizations maintain detailed profiles on Ergatas.

2

You search with purpose
Filter by country of impact, people group, job skill, sending organization, or support level to match your church's strategy.

3

Start the conversation
Use in-app anonymous messaging to learn more about workers and their ministries, and begin the vetting process.

Does your church send workers directly?

If your church sends missionaries itself, rather than through a separate sending agency, our optional Ministry Review feature gives you a simple accountability mechanism. Ask your workers for an annual budget and quarterly or annual reports — Ergatas reminds them automatically when a document is due, and your committee sees at a glance who has submitted and who is still outstanding.
