Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ergatas really free?

Yes. Creating a profile is free, and we take no platform fee and no cut of your donations. When a supporter gives, we collect the funds and pass them to your sending organization designated for your support. Some bank fees apply, but Ergatas keeps nothing — and donors can choose to cover the bank fees so 100% reaches your ministry.

How is this different from GoFundMe?

Unlike a general fundraising site, Ergatas is built for missionary support raising. There are no platform fees, donations flow through your sending agency so they stay tax-deductible and accountable, and supporters actively search Ergatas for a missionary to partner with — so you're found rather than having to drive all of your own traffic. It's designed for lasting monthly partnerships, not a one-time campaign with an end date.
