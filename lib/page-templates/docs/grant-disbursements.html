Disbursements

Disbursements is where grant staff record fund releases against a board decision, keeping them within the delegated scope the board authorized. Reach it from Grant Compliance.

Recording a disbursement here does not move money — it is an internal ledger that tracks releases against the delegated cap. (Salary reports draw their "sent for salary" figures from the organization's actual donations, not from this ledger.)

Recording a disbursement

Select the Board approval that is the source of the delegation. The page then shows the delegated scope for that approval — and any monthly cap — or warns you that no disbursement authority was delegated. Enter the:

  • Reporting period (for example, 2026-06),
  • Amount,
  • Method, and an optional Note.

Click Record disbursement. A release that exceeds the delegated cap, or that is recorded against a suspended or terminated decision, is rejected — those require a fresh board decision.
